Share a scene

SVG, GIF, PNG, WebP or JPG. Up to 300 KB for SVG and 95 KB for the others, so they fit the card in Claude Code. Fits best at 560 × 350 (wide), 560 × 560 (square) or 560 × 840 (tall). Pixel art can be drawn small, like 128 × 80.

An automatic check looks at it first. That takes a few seconds.
