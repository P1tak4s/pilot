# Progress bar

A band above the prompt that follows a bigger task step by step: it fills toward a finish flag as steps complete,
names the step in progress, turns yellow with a sound when Claude asks the person something, and green with a sound
when every step is done. Isac left the design to the builder (2026-10-06); each decision below is the builder's.

## When it appears
- **Planning**: Claude enters plan mode (`EnterPlanMode`, main loop). One row, "Planning a bigger task" with thinking
  dots. (A high effort pick alone used to show it, on a guess: a handoff at high showed Planning, then nothing.) If the turn ends without a step list, it goes away without a sound.
- **Asking for a list**: a `prompt.compose` section asks Claude to write a task list for work of 3+ steps; without it
  Claude often skipped the list and no bar came.
- **Working**: Claude writes a step list with 3 or more steps: `TodoWrite` (the whole list each time) or
  `TaskCreate` / `TaskUpdate` (one task at a time). Fewer than 3 steps draws nothing.
- Subagents' lists are ignored (`agentId` set): only the main conversation's task counts.

## What a step is
One todo or task. Progress = (completed + half of the one in progress) / total. The step named is the one in
progress, in its `activeForm` ("Running the tests"); else the next pending one.

## States
| State | Colour | Sound | Ends when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| planning | brand purple | none | a list arrives, or the turn ends (gone) |
| working | brand purple | none | the turn ends |
| asking | yellow | question chime | the person answers (AskUserQuestion result, or the next prompt) |
| paused | dim purple | none | the next prompt (back to working) |
| done | green | done chime | the next prompt, or ✕ |

At the end of a main-loop turn: every step completed is done; an answer whose end holds a question (`endsOnQuestion`)
is asking; anything else (interrupted, an error, stopped mid-list) is paused. `AskUserQuestion` turns the bar yellow
as the call starts and back once it is answered.

## Where it sits
One band at a time, as before. Priority: settings, handoff bar, setup guide, then the progress bar while it is
planning, working or asking (the person is mid-task; the alerts can wait), then judge down, running hot, cold, swamp,
then the progress bar when done or paused, then the effort row.

## Drawing
Brand surface (BRAND_BG, BRAND_EDGE, ACCENT), or the yellow / green set. Plain Box and Text only, no Svg: the bar
redraws as steps move, and an animated Svg flickers on every redraw. Row 1: `✦ effortless`, "3 of 5", the step, ✕ in
the flow. Row 2: one segment per step (filled, half filled, empty), a flag at the end; past 12 steps one continuous
bar. Below 4 rows of room it draws one row with a short bar of blocks. Key `progress-bar`.

## Sounds
Two short chimes shipped in `sounds/` (generated by `sounds/make-sounds.mjs`). `$.audio.play` plays nothing in a
Windows terminal, so on Windows the mod plays the file with PowerShell's `Media.SoundPlayer`; elsewhere
`$.audio.play({ asset })`. A sound that fails is skipped silently.

## Off switch
Two parts in `hide` and in Settings > Show: `progress` (the whole bar, no tracking, no sound) and `sounds` (the bar
stays, silent).

## Test aids
`/effortless progress` draws a demo bar (2 of 5); `progress ask` and `progress done` show the other states with
their sounds; `progress clear` removes it.

## Code
`hooks/progress.tsx` holds the state, the hooks and the drawing; `register.tsx` calls `registerProgress` once and
draws the band in its two places. Kept apart because other branches edit `register.tsx`.

## Known limits
- While the AskUserQuestion dialog is open the app may draw it in place of the band; the chime still plays.
- In split view the app draws plugin UI only in the left pane.
