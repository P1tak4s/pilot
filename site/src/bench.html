What works, and what does not

What works

Opus costs less at every setting.21% less than medium, 29% less than high, 42% less than xhigh, and every answer still right.
Fewer steps, not just less thinking.5.8 requests per task against 7.3 on medium. Every request rereads the whole chat, so fewer requests is where the saving comes from.
Faster too.20 s per task against 24 s on medium and 44 s on xhigh.
The judge is usually right.On 73 hand-labelled prompts, Haiku picks an acceptable effort 93% of the time, Jev 99%. A fixed effort manages 44 to 51%.

What does not

Sonnet at medium is already lean.Against it, effortless came out even (4% more, inside the noise). It still saved 10% against high and 35% against xhigh.
Switching models per prompt does not pay.Each model keeps its own cache, so moving one prompt to a cheaper model makes it reread the whole chat. In a chat that is in use that costs more than it saves, so this is now off by default (see Benchmark 3).
Mostly easy tasks.effortless saves most by going low on simple work. On hard work it picks more effort, and saves less.

Benchmark 3

Why model switching is off

An earlier version also moved simple prompts to Haiku or Sonnet. Measured on single prompts, the cheaper answers were good enough in 27 of 30 and cost 60 to 77% less. But those prompts started with no chat history. In a real chat the context sits in the chat model's cache, and each model keeps its own: a prompt moved to Haiku has to write the whole chat into Haiku's cache first, at twice the input price. From a warm Opus chat that costs about 5 times what staying on Opus does (10 times for Sonnet).

So: "Cheaper model when it can" is now off by default and marked experimental. The saving that holds up in real chats is effort, Benchmark 1.

Limits

Small set

Four tasks, three runs each. The percentages could move a few points on a rerun.

Mostly easy work

effortless saves most on simple tasks, where it goes low. On hard tasks it picks more effort and saves less; that was not measured here.

Checks, not taste

Each answer was checked for the right fact, file or number. Not for how well it was written.

API, fresh chats

Run through Anthropic's API in fresh chats. Long chats in the app, and plan usage, were not measured.
