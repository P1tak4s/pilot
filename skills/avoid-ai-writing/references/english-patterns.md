## Structural Patterns to Avoid

### Template sentences
- "In today's rapidly evolving [X]..."
- "In an era where [X]..."
- "As we navigate [X]..."
- "X has never been more important"
- "It is essential to note that..."
- "It goes without saying that..."

### Hollow intensifiers
- "truly", "really", "very", "quite", "rather" - cut them
- "absolutely", "certainly", "undoubtedly" - cut them

### Hedging chains
- "it could be argued that"
- "one might say"
- "in some ways"
- "to some extent"
- "more or less"

### Rule of three (formulaic)
- "X, Y, and Z" when listing three adjectives in a row to sound comprehensive
- Ending with a positive third item: "challenges remain, but X, Y, and Z"

### Negative parallelism
- "Not only X, but also Y"
- "It's not X, it's Y"
- "Not just X, but Y"

### Transition words overused by AI
- "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally", "Nevertheless", "Consequently"
- "In conclusion", "To summarize", "Overall", "In essence", "Ultimately"

### Copula avoidance
AI avoids "is/are" and substitutes: "serves as", "stands as", "represents", "marks", "reflects"
Fix: use "is" or restructure the sentence.

### Significance inflation
- "plays a pivotal role in"
- "has had a profound impact on"
- "serves as a testament to"
- "indelible mark on"
- "reflects broader trends"
- "marking a new chapter"

### Superficial -ing analysis
Appended present-participle phrases that add no information:
- "highlighting the importance of X"
- "underscoring the significance of Y"
- "contributing to the overall understanding of Z"

### Formulaic challenges section
- "Despite these achievements, [subject] faces challenges..."
- "While significant progress has been made..."
- "Looking ahead, there are opportunities to..."

### Vague attributions
- "industry experts say"
- "researchers argue"
- "some critics suggest"
- "modern scholars believe"
Fix: name the source or cut the claim.

### Generic conclusions
- "The future remains bright for..."
- "Only time will tell..."
- "This is just the beginning..."
- "As we move forward..."
- "The possibilities are endless"

### Chatbot artifacts
- "As an AI language model..."
- "As of my knowledge cutoff..."
- "I should note that..."
- "It's important to acknowledge..."
- "I want to be transparent..."

## Formatting Patterns to Avoid

- **Em dash overuse** - using - instead of commas or just restructuring
- **Excessive bold**: bolding every noun or key term mechanically
- **Bullet-heavy sections**: converting prose into bullets when the ideas connect
- **Emoji as section headers**: ✅ What works / ❌ What doesn't
- **Title Case In Every Heading**: capitalize only first word
- **"Key takeaways" box**: inherited from sales decks
- **Inline-header lists**: **Bold phrase**: supporting text (repeated as bullets)

## Second-Pass Check

After rewriting, scan for:
1. Any word from the replacement table surviving
2. Sentences that start with "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally"
3. The phrase "it's worth noting" or "importantly"
4. Any sentence ending in "...highlighting/underscoring/demonstrating the importance of..."
5. Three-adjective lists
6. Generic conclusion sentences
